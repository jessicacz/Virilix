REVIEWS & INFORMATION

Look for context, not just star ratings.

A useful review considers what product version was used, what expectations were held and what other factors were changing at the same time.

REVIEW FRAMEWORK

Separate experience from evidence.

Individual experiences can describe how someone perceived a product, but they do not establish what a supplement will do for everyone.

When reading reviews, look for product version, usage context, timeframe and whether other lifestyle factors changed.

What responsible product information looks like.

Claims about dramatic medical or performance outcomes should be treated cautiously unless they are supported by appropriate evidence.

Marketing language can explain how a product is positioned, while scientific evidence addresses what can actually be established. Those are not interchangeable.

This website is informational. Always refer to current official product information, the current label and appropriate professional guidance for individual circumstances.
